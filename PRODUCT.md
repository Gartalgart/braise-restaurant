# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** diners near Lille, mostly on their phone, often one-handed and on the move, deciding where to eat tonight or this weekend. Their job: check that the place appeals to them, look at the menu (diet, allergens, price), then book a table in under 30 seconds.
- **Secondary (portfolio context):** independent restaurant owners being pitched by the developer. They judge whether this kind of site would bring them direct bookings without a commission-taking platform.

## Product Purpose

A showcase site for an independent restaurant that turns visits into direct bookings. Every page pushes toward booking a table and makes visitors hungry. Success means a visitor finds a real slot and confirms it from a phone in under 30 seconds, with no third-party app.

## Positioning

Braise is a small 30-cover restaurant where everything is cooked over a beech and oak wood fire. Its short menu follows the Wazemmes market and the seasons of northern France. The site's edge over booking platforms: real-time slots straight from the restaurant's database, no commission, and a menu the owner can edit themselves.

## Operating Context

- **Pages:** Accueil, Menu (filterable by category, diet, allergens and price), Réservation (step 1: day, party size, slot; step 2: name, email, phone; then confirmation), Galerie (lightbox), Avis (Google rating and excerpts), Contact & horaires (map).
- **Hours:** stored in `lib/restaurant.ts`. Closed on Mondays and on Sunday evenings.
- **Groups of 7 or more:** prepayment of €10 per person (Stripe is optional and not wired up).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack to keep:** Next.js 15 App Router, Tailwind CSS 4, Motion (Framer Motion), Prisma + PostgreSQL (Docker), `next/image`.
- **Business logic to keep intact:** `/api/creneaux`, `/api/reservations`, the Prisma schema, filter logic and slot management. The redesign changes presentation only.
- **Data:**
  - Menu of 16 dishes in `prisma/seed.ts`.
  - Slots generated by the seed over 30 days.
  - Gallery in `lib/gallery.ts`.
  - Reviews in `lib/reviews.ts`.
- **Accessibility and use:** fully responsive, mobile-first, booking flow usable from the keyboard.
- **3D:** allowed, but at most one element and only if it serves the ambiance. It must be lazy-loaded with a static fallback.

## Brand Commitments

- The name **Braise** is kept.
- No other visual or verbal commitment has been made binding (palette, voice, typography and menu layout are all open for the redesign).

## Evidence on Hand

- **Status: fictional demo restaurant for a portfolio.** Address, phone, reviews, the 4.8/5 rating from 327 reviews, and the "Hauts-de-France" claims are illustrative content.
- **Menu:** `prisma/seed.ts` (no burger on it). The hero and photos must only show dishes from this menu, or the dining room.
- **Photos:** free stock photos (Unsplash). For the redesign, choose them to match the actual dishes, and make every alt text describe what the photo really shows.
- **Must not be fabricated beyond the demo's scope:** press coverage, awards, chef names, partnerships.

## Product Principles

1. **Booking first.** A way to book is reachable from every screen, including the first screen on mobile.
2. **Make them hungry with the truth.** Show dishes that are really on the menu, the fire and the room, never a product the restaurant doesn't serve.
3. **Thirty seconds, one thumb.** Three decisions, then three fields. Every state (closed, full, error) points to a way forward.
4. **The owner keeps control.** Menu, hours and slots stay easy to change, with no developer needed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast of at least 4.5:1, touch targets of 44 px, visible focus.
- Allergen information must stay readable (it is safety information).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` without losing state feedback.
